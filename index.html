Our Story & Legacy

Stefan was born in Poland and graduated from a leather vocational school in 1965, training in the traditional craft of leatherwork and shoe repair from an early age. He immigrated to Baltimore in 1983, bringing decades of old-world skill with him.

He started as a cobbler at Reisterstown Plaza before opening his first shop on Roland Avenue in 1992. In 2007, the shop moved to its current home at 3810 Falls Road in Hampden, where it has served the neighborhood ever since.

Today, Stefan's two sons — who hold master's degrees in computer science and electrical engineering — help carry the family legacy forward, combining generations of hand-craftsmanship with a modern eye for the business.
